Everyone has to submit oneself to an unconditional level of Nature. *This is his stronghold, this his root in an enduring reality, and with this security he can go out into the world, not only to settle the old imperial demands, but also, perchance, to reanimate the forms that ire with the vision of what is to be*[^1]

Carl Jung induced the existence of Nature through the observation by the simple fact that two young children can present two different attitudes while being subject to the same environment[^2]

Date: 1923
