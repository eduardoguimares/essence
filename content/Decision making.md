# Types of decisions

- **Reduced** - foreseen technical decisions which tend to be restricted
- **Fluid** - non-controversial decisions, where its information is trustworthy and easy to obtain
	- Tend to be supported by complex systems and made by superiors
- **Sporadic** - Qualitative, intuitive and non-structured decisions that are impactful and controversial
	- Information to make the decision is not usually accessible
	- It involves the 'political game'

## Political influence

Organizations are arenas of interests where different factions with different goals emerge. Coalitions are set up, the most dominant political forces influence and limit behaviors and actions and there's the possibility of making the decision of not deciding anything

# How to improve decision-making

How to limit the effects of [[Bounded rationality]][^1]

- Using technology
- Working in a team with diverse capabilities, expertise and experience
	- Fills knowledge gaps
	- Reduces the time and effort the team needs to exert on the decision
		- More in-depth research
- Using a choice architecture called [[Nudge theory|nudges]] to help people make optimal decisions

# How to measure decision quality

The quality of your decisions must not be measured by the results but by the process that led to the final decision

The optimal decision is the one that makes the best use of the information available in the time limits. You could spend all the time gathering all the information and never make a decision. Or, at the other extreme, you could make an instant decision without any information. There's a [[Trade-off]] and there's a decision involved about how you're going to make the decision

Also, there's [[Risk context|risk]] involved and it's important to be able to make decisions without having all the relevant information
