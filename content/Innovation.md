Innovation in a business context refers to the ability to conceive, develop, deliver, and scale new products, services, processes, and business models for customers. It extends beyond mere [[Creativity]] and the development of new products. It aims for substantial new net growth

# Essential elements of innovation

- Who - unmet customer needs
- What - solution
- How - business model
- Why - value and growth
	- Successful innovation must increase value and drive growth

# Attributes of innovation

- Strategic vision innovators have a clear vision aligned with market trends and customer needs
- Cross-functional collaboration innovations thrive when diverse teams collaborate across disciplines
- Risk tolerance innovators embrace calculated risks and learn from failures
- Iterative process innovators iterate, learn and adapt based on feedback

# The innovation process

A systematic approach that organizations use to develop new products, services, or processes that deliver value to customers and stakeholders. It usually includes [[Design thinking]] in the process

1. Idea generation
2. Concept development ideas are evaluated and developed into more detailed concepts
3. Feasibility analysis the practicality and viability of the concepts are assessed
4. Prototype development a working model or prototype is created to test the concept
5. Testing and validation the prototype is tested and refined based on feedback
6. Implementation the final product or service is launched and commercialized
7. Scale-up the innovation is scaled up for mass production and distribution
8. Evaluation and feedback
9. The process can then start over again for constant improvement
