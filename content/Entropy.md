It refers to the randomness and organization of a system
- Low entropy = low randomness, high organization, low complexity
- High entropy = high randomness low organization, low complexity

High complexity is in the middle of the low and high entropy

Second Law of thermodynamics: there is a natural tendency of things in the Universe going from low entropy to high entropy. The energy spreads out over time

Past hypothesis: It is believed that the level of entropy of the Universe has been increasing since the Big Bang
