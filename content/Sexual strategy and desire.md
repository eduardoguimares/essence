# The female sexual strategy

*Female physiological arousal (as measured by vaginal lubrication) occurs in response to viewing most any type of sexual activity: man with woman, woman with woman, man with man. Even watching sex among Bonobo monkeys stimulates physiological arousal in women.*[^2]

*...the vagina immediately becomes moist at any hint of sexual activity in the vicinity so as to protect the woman from injury in the event of rape or sexual violence. This arousal is not necessarily related to the sexual desires, intents, or preferences of the woman.*[^2]

Clearly, women's biological and psychological state of desire works very different to men. So what does women really want?

Maybe intimacy and emotional connection with a very specific partner. Women don't have seed to spread, they are much more vulnerable and they take more time to reach orgasm, so it makes sense that they are very careful when choosing sexual partners. Which makes me think that women tend to prefer intimate, closed, stable relationships to feel aroused. I mean, *there is virtually no female sexual problem—hormonal, menopausal, orgasmic, or just plain old lack of interest—that will not be solved by—ta-da!—a new lover*[^2]. A marriage provides security, intimacy and help with the children.
Maybe sexual activity with multiple partners like men. Some studies show that when women feel safe about what they say, their opinion about desire for sex tends to be the same as men[^2]. Also, why would men have fear of potential cuckoldry?

Moreover, *human sexuality is adapted for sperm competition*[^2]. It would make sense for woman to have sex with multiple partners in the most fertile period so their sperms compete. Men's sexuality are also adapt to this perspective.

Maybe *women initiate divorce more often than men, and benefit less from marriage than do men on measures of health, happiness, and wealth*

- *Female desire, according to Meana, is activated when a woman feels overwhelmingly desired, not rationally considered*
