It is not just a set of shared values ([[Organizational culture]]). It’s a dynamic force that shapes behavior, influences decision-making, and defines the very essence of an organization

Organizations actively manage their identity to align with their desired image, values, and goals. To do that, organizations manage social practices in order to affect the construction of the identity. This process is called identity regulation and it influences how employees perceive themselves as organizational members

The social practices implemented by the organizations should maintain a balance between consistency and adaptability. Consistency to build trust and recognition. Adaptability to to remain relevant

# Strategies for identity regulation

## Human Resource Management processes

1. Recruitment and selection - Human Resource Management makes sure these processes align with the organization's identity
2. Onboarding - new employees are socialize into the organization's culture, norms and values
3. Mentoring and role modeling - experienced employees reinforce the organizational identity
4. Training and development - it covers skills, behaviors and norms that reinforce the identity
5. Identity alignment evaluation - Human Resource Management can evaluate the performance of the individual in order to assess how well they embodied the company's identity
6. Employee engagement - to foster a sense of belonging

## Leadership

Vision and mission statements

Leadership behavior - leading by example. Just like Heroic [[Leadership]]

## Symbolic practices

Branding (visual symbols like logos, shapes and colors) employees associate them with their identity as part of the organization

Rituals and ceremonies they create a sense of continuity and belonging
