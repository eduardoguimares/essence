*Enterprise [[System|systems]] are large-scale application software packages that support business processes, information flows, reporting, and data analytics in complex [[Organization|organizations]]*[^1]. They integrate the internal business processes (operations, accounting, finance, human resources, marketing and sales, etc.) into a single platform to make access to key data and completing work process easier

# Types of enterprise systems

## Enterprise resource planning

ERP integrates an enterprise's internal applications and business processes, supports its external business processes and links to its external business partners

ERP is used primarily in the manufacturing industry

## Supply Chain Management

*Supply chain management is the process of managing the flow of goods and services to and from a business, including every step involved in turning raw materials and components into final products and getting them to the ultimate customer*[^2]

It involves, procurement, sourcing, manufacturing, storage, inventory control, scheduling, order fulfillment and distribution

## Customer Relationship Management

Customer relationship management is a system for managing all of the company’s interactions with current and potential customers. It involves a business strategy to segment and manage customers to optimize customer lifetime value

## Knowledge management system

A [[Knowledge]] Management System (KMS) harnesses an organization’s collective knowledge about business productivity, competitive business models and [[Business Intelligence]], leading to better operational efficiencies. These systems are supported by a knowledge base, which serves as a centralized repository for information

> [!a] Example
> Intranets, wikis, databases

### Types of KMS

- Internal KMS
- Enterprise KMS
- Document Management System
- External KMS
- Customer Support Knowledge Base

## Content management system

A CMS is computer software used to manage the creation and modification of digital content

### Enterprise content management system

A ECMS provides capability for multiple users throughout an organization with different permission levels to manage content and documents related to organizational processes

### Document management system

A DMS is a computerized system used to store, share, track and manage files or documents, also known as digital [[Archiving]]

### Web content management system

A WCMS is a CMS specifically for the [[Web]]

## Enterprise social platform

ESP refers to a platform that enhances social networks, both within the enterprise and across key members of the enterprise's supply and distribution chains. It can include private social networks, software or technology enhances communication, coordination and collaboration within an organization
