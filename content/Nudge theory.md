Nudge theory is a concept *that proposes designs of the decision-making environment as ways to influence the behavior and decision-making of groups and individuals*[^1]

*A nudge makes it more likely that an individual will make a particular choice, or behave in a particular way*[^1] by affecting the individual's system 1[^2]

[^2]: Title: *Thinking, fast and slow*
