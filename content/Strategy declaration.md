Very present in companies through Marketing and [[Strategy]]

# Components

- Vision - mental picture of what the organization and society could become
- Purpose - why doing what it is intended to do
- Mission - the general committed duty that will bring the vision into reality
- Goals - broad, long-term milestones necessary to get closer to the vision
- Objectives - specific, realistic and measurable targets that help directing actions towards the goals
- Principle/Values - belief system that is the basis of the decisions
- Context of the activities
	- Organization sector
- Unique position (advantages and skills available) that one has to achieve its goals and objectives

# Another perspective

We can use [[Cognitive function#Cognitive sequences|cognitive sequences]] to better visualize some of the components

In the case of judgement sequences, Ti > Te (process) > Fi (values) > Fe (purpose)

The purpose indicates the positive contribution one wants to have in the world. The values are one's direction of behaviour - what do you emotionally believe in. The process allows you to define the tactics. The mission is a pattern in the tactics in place
