The SECI model of knowledge management, developed by Ikujiro Nonaka and Hirotaka Takeuchi, describes how organizations create and manage knowledge through a dynamic interplay between tacit and explicit knowledge

![[SECI model]]
# Socialization

Socialization enables individual tacit knowledge to be shared and to become collective

Language isn't used. Instead observation, imitation and practice are used. That's what allows leading by example in the Post-heroic [[Leadership]] style. That's how novice learn form the master -> through this type of direct contact

%%Curso de comandos por exemplo%%

It's very specific to the culture and very local. Therefore, it's difficult to share beyond its immediate tacit context of emergence

# Externalization

Articulates tacit knowledge into explicit knowledge. Knowledge is codified into concepts, models, theories, metaphors or analogies

# Combination

Aims to combine different bodies of explicit knowledge together to systematize shared concepts into a knowledge system

New explicit knowledge is created -> sorted, categorized, re contextualized. Combination relies on networks, databases and information

# Internalization

Enables the conversion of explicit knowledge into embodied tacit knowledge. This process is traditionally referred to as [[Learning]]

# Organizational knowledge creation cycle

1. Sharing tacit knowledge
2. Concepts creation
3. Concept justification
4. Archetype building
5. Cross-levelling knowledge
	- The knowledge becomes organizational and a new cycle begins

The knowledge accumulates on the previous cycle forming a spiral
