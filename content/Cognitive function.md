# Cognitive sequences

Typical sequence: Input > Process > Output > Feedback

Judgement sequence: [[Introverted thinking|Ti]] > [[Extroverted thinking|Te]] > [[Introverted feeling|Fi]] > [[Extroverted feeling|Fe]]

Perception sequence: [[Extroverted intuition|Ne]] > [[Introverted intuition|Ni]] > [[Extroverted sensing|Se]] > [[Introverted sensing|Si]]

The processes of the human psyche are not that linear. It's usually messy and lacking one of the steps. Also, these processes can happen at a micro scale (individually) or at a larger scale - potentially more efficient

Ti develops logic pieces based on information gathered. It starts by creating a web of nodes with defined directions (Ne) and setting the probability of energy transfer between them (Ni). It results in a [[Markov Chain]] which will produce effects in the nodes - the energy will flow between the nodes (Se) and they might or might not change their states (Si) - creates a piece of [[information]]

Te constructs a rational structure with the logic pieces. It starts by creating an hypothesis with the acquired information (Ne) and setting the stage in which the hypothesis can be observed (Ni) - plan. It leads to a change in the condition of the targeted structure (Se) which is interpreted and categorized (Si) - creates [[knowledge]]

Changes in the condition cause emotional reactions. These reactions It starts by testing how the subject would feel in the resulted conditions (Ne)

Fe adapts itself according to the values of the majority
