# Neo-normative control

Neo-normative control refers to the understanding of how norms and standards shape organizational behavior. These norms act as guiding principles that influence how individuals and groups operate within an organization. It shapes individuals and the [[Organizational identity]]

## Explicit norms

Formalized rules explicitly communicated within the organization

> [!a] Examples
> Dress code policies
Attendance policies
Code of conduct
Laws

## Implicit norms

Unspoken expectations that emerge from organizational culture and interactions. They are often subtle but powerful

> [!a] Examples
> Social norms
Communication style
Work hours
