Creativity is the ability to generate novel and valuable ideas, solutions, or products. It requires [[Intermingleness]]

# Creative people

*The common trait of people who supposedly have vision is that they spend a lot of time reading and gathering information, and then they synthesize it until they come up with an idea*[^2]

Creative individuals have the capacity to flow between one's [[Unconscious]] and [[Subconscious]]

## Traits[^1]

- They have a lot of physical energy, but are also often quiet or at rest. They control their energy and how to make it flow
- They are smart and naïve
- They have both playfulness and responsibility
- They are both down to earth and imaginative
- They are both extroverted and introverted
- They are both humble and proud
- They to an extent escape rigid gender role stereotyping
- They are both rebellious and conservative
- They are both very passionate and objective about their work
- Their openness and sensitivity often exposes them to suffering and pain, yet also to a great deal of enjoyment

# Creativity in organizations

## How to enhance creativity in organizations

- Create a psychologically safe environment - shape organizational climate to one that supports risk-taking and experimentation
	- Design workspaces that inspire creativity
		- Through open layouts, comfortable seating and art installations
- Create a diverse team with different backgrounds, experiences and viewpoints
	- That way, the team will most likely tend to challenge conventional thinking
	- That way, it encourages collaboration
- Choose the right individuals for creative roles
	- Not forgetting to consider their function within a team
- Choose a creative [[Leadership|Leader]] who is good at leading creative teams and individuals
	- Who encourages and champions creative ideas
	- Who communicates the importance of creativity
	- Who sets expectations for innovative thinking
	- Who facilitates problem-solving within teams
- Implement training and development programs that enhance individual and team creativity
- Implement creative techniques
	- Brainstorming
	- Mind mapping
	- [[Design thinking]]
- Encourage and give space to employees to spend part of their work time on personal projects
	- Example: Google encourages employees to spend 20% of their work time on personal projects. This policy has led to innovations like Gmail and Google News
- Implement financial incentives for useful ideas
- Allocate resources to support creative endeavors

# Why creating

It's an effective way of [[Learning]]

When paired with publishing, it allows us to create a narrative about ourselves[^3] and to build a resume. The content has a unique flavor because it involves our own perspective and way of presenting

By sharing, it opens opportunities for new connections and contributes to a public conversation[^3]
