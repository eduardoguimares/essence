This theory explains how people bond with each other[^1]

# In children

- Secure attachment
- Anxious-ambivalent attachment
- Anxious-avoidant and dismissive-avoidant attachment
- Disorganized/disoriented attachment

# In adults

- Securely attached
- Anxious-preoccupied
- Dismissive-avoidant
- Fearful-avoidant

[^1]: John Bowlby
