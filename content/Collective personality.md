| Temples | Side of the mind        | Main functions |
| ------- | ----------------------- | -------------- |
| Body    | [[Ego]]                 | Se, Ti         |
| Mind    | [[Subconscious]]        | Te, Si         |
| Heart   | [[Unconscious]]         | Ni, Fe         |
| Soul    | [[Super-ego\|Superego]] | Fi, Ne         |

From the main functions of each temple, it's possible to deduct

[[Extroverted sensing|Se]] dominant
[[Introverted thinking|Ti]] parent
[[Extroverted thinking|Te]] child - societies are always drawn to create their own interpretations of events ([[Sensemaking]]) - when a common interpretation is in place, a sense of togetherness comes into play
[[Introverted sensing|Si]] fear - societies always have a dark past; in order to aspire greatness, one has to embrace it
[[Introverted intuition|Ni]] nemesis
[[Extroverted feeling|Fe]] critic - societies are very sensitive to each others ethics; this is a defense mechanism to increase membership cohesion
[[Introverted feeling|Fi]] trickster - societies are blind to their moral compass because the collective well-being is the priority in critical situations. When the culture is not healthy and is collapsing, a loud minority has to rise up and become the new default
[[Extroverted intuition|Ne]] demon - in critical moments of History we can observe societies going rogue and collapsing; a chaotic minority transforms into the major force and causes a revolution for the sake of opening up to new possibilities
