[[Data]] management is the management of the flow of data from its creation and initial storage until the moment the data is obsolete and excluded, which can include the [[ETL process]]

[[Information]] management is slightly different. It is the process of collecting storing, managing and keeping accurate, useful, trustworthy, available, exclusive and relevant data

There's some overlap on both processes

# Benefits of data management in organizations

- Lower costs
	- Lower data storage costs
	- Lower localization and integration costs
- Better [[Data analytics]] performance
- Better data security
	- Better [[Digital privacy]]
	- Few legal troubles
- Better data precision and consistency
	- Better decision-making
- Better product classification
- Better marketing and sales strategies

# Challenges

- Disperse data - data can be disperse through different businesses and stored in different systems used for different purposes
	- It makes it more difficult for the user to access all the information that they need
- The silo effect from [[Data silo]]s
	- To go from silos to collaboration, it requires a cultural change
- 'Garbage in, garbage out' - *If input data are not complete, accurate, and timely, then the resulting output is unreliable and of no useful value*[^1]. The bad quality data is called 'dirty data'
	- To avoid that, the data needs to be validated
		- For example, when an email is inserted in a form and it checks if the email is correctly formatted before it is sent
	- This can lead to useless terabytes of data
		- The decision-maker will have a lot of uncertainty making the decisions
	- It can lead to [[Data Chaos]] which can put an organization in risk


[^1]: *Garbage in, garbage out*, Wordnik dictionary
