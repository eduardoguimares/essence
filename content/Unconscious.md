The compensatory force of the conscious side of the mind. The senses of the unconscious *take on a regressive character corresponding with the degree of their repression*[^1] - *the less they are recognized, the more infantile and archaic they become.*[^1]

*The compensating attitude of the unconscious finds expression in the process of psychic equilibrium*[^1]. When the conscious is repressed, the unconscious is presented as a symptom

The unconscious uses the remainder energy not used by the conscious

You don't need to 'excavate' too deep to find the unconscious. *On the contrary, there is a constant influx of the unconscious into the conscious psychological  process*[^1]


[^1]: Title: *Psychological Types* 
Author: C. G. Jung
Date: 1923
