## Development

During childhood, the child directs his attention to his parents or tutors. If there's compatibility, the child will become subconscious developed (SD). If not, then unconscious developed (UD). It is believed that development can not be changed after childhood.

The SD child tends to prefer a mother figure. The UD child tends to prefer a father figure.

The SD child prioritizes thrival (social instincts) over survival (individual instincts). The UD child prioritizes survival over thrival. (see the different ends of the [[Deviance Continuum]])

## Focus

During adolescence, the SD teen seeks to come out of his parents' protective bubble and starts using the thoughts of his peers as his anchor of perspective. The teen focuses on engaging and dealing with his deep worries and cynism about the established world. If his thoughts are relaxed by a compatible peer, then he becomes subconscious focused (SF). If his unconscious is enabled by his peers, then he becomes unconscious focused.

The UD teen seeks to come out of his parents' challenging environment and tries to find peers to explore more of his conscious. If the teen finds a compatible peer, he becomes SF. If the teen continues to not find a compatible peer, he becomes UF.

It is believed that focus is situational and has some degree of flexibility in order to adapt to life's challenges. Therefore, even though focus on the opposite side is stronger in adolescence years, during adulthood focus can adapt to life circumstances.
