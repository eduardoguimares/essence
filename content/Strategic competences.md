Strategic competences are competences that position the organization in an unique position and allow the organization to get a competitive advantage

# Resources perspective

Strategic competences should be designed according to the resources available
  
| Nature    | Resources                                                           | Competences                                              |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Physic    | Machines, buildings, materials, products, patents, data, IT systems | Efficiency, productivity, flexibility, comercialization  |
| Financial | Balance, cash-flow, loan access, funding                            | Capacity of obtaining capital, cash-flow management      |
| Human     | Managers, collaborators, partners, suppliers, clients               | Skills, knowledge, relationships, motivation, innovation |

# Dynamic competences

The capacity of an organization to renovate and recreate its own strategic competences in order to respond to the changing environment
