# New breath, new me [^1]

Whenever you inhale and exhale, you become a new self. Therefore, you don't need to feel bad about the mistakes you made in the past, because those mistakes were made by different versions of yourself. However, you also can't justify yourself by what you were in the past. If your previous versions were good people, it doesn't mean that the current version is also good. It's important to ensure the continuity of positive characteristics while eliminating negative ones.

[^1]: Marshall Goldsmith
