The [[Rationality|rational]] [[Decision making]] [[model]] is a mainstream view of decision making and it considers that decision making starts with a problem and it ends with a solution

# Steps

0. Problem diagnosis - recognizing, defining and characterizing the problem
1. Collecting information
2. Generate alternative solutions
3. Compare solutions using a specific criteria
4. Choose the best solution
5. Implement the solution
6. Monitor the impact of the solution
