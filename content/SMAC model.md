The intersection between 4 digital marketing philosophies: Social, Mobile, Analytics, Cloud

- Social
	- Networking (social media)
	- Commerce
- Mobile - smart technologies that access the Internet and are handheld (phone, tablet, watch)
- [[Data analytics]]
- Cloud - Internet platform
	- A philosophy that views the internet as more than a source of information but as a platform with which to build speedy digital apps and websites

The SMAC [[model]] is a tool used by many businesses seeking digital transformation. Nowadays, the understanding of how important digital platforms are is so prevalent and most of the digital transformation is already made that CIO's don't feel the need for a model to guide them
