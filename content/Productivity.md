# Time vs energy

It's useful to consider energy management in addition to time management, since it is a very relevant variable

# The top heavy approach

Use the top heavy approach[^1]
1. Work more in the morning vs the afternoon
2. Work more in the early weeks of a project
3. Work more in the early days of the week

Basically, put more weight in the beginning vs the end. It is essential to have a lot of time to relax at the end, so you can work intensively in the beginning


[^1]: *Finish Work Faster by Copying How Olympic Athletes Train*, Scott Young (2011)
