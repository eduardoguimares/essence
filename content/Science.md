*The public has a distorted view of science because children are [[Education|taught in school]] that science is a collection of firmly established truths. In fact, science is not a collection of truths. It is a continuing exploration of mysteries*[^1]

*Ridicule is not a part of the scientific method and the public should not be taught that it is*[^2]

[^1]: Freeman Dyson
[^2]: J. Allen Hynek
