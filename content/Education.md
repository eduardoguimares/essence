# Education principles

*A teacher doesn't teach what one says, the teacher transmits what is*[^6]

# Education design

*The trick to education is to teach people in such a way that they don't realize they're learning until it's to late*[^4]

*Schools should include a class called Truth Is Hard, where you get bombarded with examples of confused eyewitnesses, incorrect public outrages, studies that failed to replicate, super convincing arguments that fall apart with one additional fact you didn't expect, etc.*[^5]

*What the pupil must learn, if he learns anything at all, is that the world will do most of the work for you, provided you cooperate with it by identifying how it really works and aligning with those realities. If we do not let the world teach us, it teaches us a lesson.*[^7]

Instead of a structured controlled approach of retaining, recalling and applying information -> learning along the way and just dive in

Using [[Artificial Intelligence|AI]] to support the learning of a student. Specific questions about a paper can be asked and the AI will provide a personalized answer according to the student's background. The AI can also make sure the learned material is recalled in the right intervals that will allow the student to retain the information ([[Spaced repetition]])

A school should help students figure out what they want to know and what they should know

## School as a business

Nowadays, knowledge is the product and students are the clients. But knowledge as become cheap. Schools, instead, should approach their business with the student both as the client and the product. The product is the transformed student
