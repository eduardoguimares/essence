AI refers to the machines or computational systems that replicate the Human intelligence

![[Artificial Intelligence types|700]]

[[Artificial Intelligence]] - tries to replicate the human intelligence
[[Machine Learning]] - uses data to train computational models
[[Deep Learning]] - tries to imitate the functioning of human neurons
**Generative AI** - has the capacity of understanding the human discourse and create content. Generative models aim to approximate to the real distribution of probability of the data in analysis

# Types

**Artificial Narrow Intelligence** ('Weak AI') - when a machine can perform a specific task

**Artificial General Intelligence** - when a machine as the capacity to comprehend and pick up any sort of skill a human can

**Artificial Super Intelligence** - when a machine surpasses human intelligence
