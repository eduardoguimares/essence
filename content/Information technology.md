Information technology is the technological component of the [[Information system]]s that allows for the collection, processing, archiving, analysis and dissemination of information

# IT in organizations

## Importance

- Allows for automatization of existent processes
- Allows to process large quantities of data and information
- Allows for the expansion and diffusion of knowledge and wisdom
- Provides instruments to support decision-making
- Makes communication more dynamic
- Helps collaboration
- Improves efficiency and productivity
- Facilitates quality improvement
- Creates competitive advantage
