# New management practices

- Agile management being flexible and responsive to change
	- It involves iterative planning and development, where projects are broken down into smaller, manageable tasks
- Emphasis on work-life balance
- Continuous learning and development - [[Mentality#Kaizen philosophy|Kaizen philosophy]]
- Diversity, equity and inclusion
- Sustainability and corporate social responsibility
- Employee empowerment and engagement
