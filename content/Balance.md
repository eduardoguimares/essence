# Balance in life

Imagine a scale balancing two trays. They're both empty and exactly and the same height. You put an object in one of the trays and another one with a different weight in the other tray. The trays will oscillate until they stabilize

When the trays were empty, was the scale balanced? Everyone would say yes. After the trays stabilized, did the scale become balanced? In the day-to-day it's easy to assume that a property should be null in order to be balanced

For example, a person has a eye misalignment and that causes some awkwardness socially. If the person could just make their eyes aligned, everything would be in balance. Or the person could learn to live with their misaligned eyes and find balance in that way

When the trays were oscillating, was the scale balanced? According to the laws of Physics everything was processed in an expected way, even if we don't completely understand. In life, we usually find ourselves oscillating between different mental states. Oscillation of natural properties is what defines [[Life]]

Is there balance in life? In the sense that the natural properties and completely stable, no. Only in the sense that you learn how to live with the oscillation of your natural and external properties. In this case, it's useful to consider a [[Binary-Range|spectrum]] of more and less balance. The highest level of balance becomes then "the optimal ability to live with the oscillations of internal and external properties"

## Chinese philosophy

*You must gain balance within yourself before you can bring balance to the world*[^2]. What it really means is that you must improve your level of **control** of your state before and make it more stable. The Chinese [[philosophy]] prioritizes what you can control and internal stability
