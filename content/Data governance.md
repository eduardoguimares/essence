[[Data]] governance is the general management of the availability, usability, integrity, and security of the data used in an organization. It requires a [[Data management]] program that includes a set of procedures and a plan to execute those procedures

# Benefits of data governance in organizations

- Avoids data errors
	- Avoids duplication of data because of isolated data
	- Avoids data inconsistency in its meaning and level of detail
	- Users can trust the data and don't need to check their credibility
- Better regulatory conformity
- More efficient and faster decisions based on reality and not on perception
- Better understanding of the data
	- Easier to determine the source of the data - who holds and is responsible for the data
- More revenue - better and faster decisions lead to less errors and [[data privacy]] violation
