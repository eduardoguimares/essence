*Business Intelligence is about delivering relevant and reliable information to the right people at the right time with the goal of achieving better decisions faster.* BI uses *methods and programs to collect and structure data, convert it into information and present it to improve business decisions*[^1]

BI is a complex system of tools and techniques that involves performance management, analytics, predictive modeling, data and text mining and [[Information system]]s

It provides the user with the requested answers and access to organized data. That way, users don't need *navigate though complex webs of linked spreadsheets, (analyse) the data manually (or to mash) together reports*[^1]

# Main tools

**[[Data mining]]**

**Data visualization** - graphic representation of data with the goal of making the understanding of concepts easier and to facilitate the identification of data patterns

**Digital dashboards** - electronic interfaces, static or interactive, used for the collection and consolidation of data

**Mashups** - combine data and applications from two or more sources, allow you to maximise the interactivity of digital panels, are a better economic solution for many problems in data analytics and allow the users to filter the information according to their needs
