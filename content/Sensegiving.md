# Sensegiving in organizations

*Narratives that explain what is going on against the background of relevant alternatives and appropriate discourses that guide how employees form their expectations*[^1]

Sensegiving uses storytelling to
- Mobilize people to change
- Clarify the change process
- Reduce anxiety and uncertainty
- Helps people make sense of their environment

## Resistance to change

People might feel that change is unnecessary, excessive and inappropriate which would require a constructive dialog and trust relationships
