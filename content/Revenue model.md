*A revenue [[model]] is a plan for earning revenue from a business or project. It explains different mechanisms of revenue generation and its sources*[^1]. It answers the questions: How to bring in revenue? Which revenue streams to create?

The revenue model is part of the [[Business model]]

# Revenue streams

- One-time purchase
- Subscription/recurring payment
- Pay-per-use
- Freemium/upseeling
- Hybrid pricing
	- A freemium plan could integrate a form of a pay-per-use tiered plan
- Ad-based - the business provides ad spaces online
- Commission-based - the business charges per marketplace or transaction
	- In the form of a flat rate, a fixed sum of money per transaction
	- In the form of a variable rate, charging a percentage per transaction
	- In the form of a tiered commission, a variable or fixed rate that changes based on the transaction volume
- Markup/profit margin - buying a product at a certain price and selling it at a higher cost
- Affiliate - redirecting customers to the providers of products or services and earning a commission on any resulting sale
- Interest-based - revenue generation in the form of interest resulted from a loan or deposit payment
- Donation-based
	- It requires loyal fans
	- It can be too unstable
