# In organizations

[[Information]] systems helps organizations being successful by
- Connecting the different parts of an organization
- Connecting the organization to its consumers and suppliers
- Helping people make better decisions
- Essentially, it improves communication

IS's are processes used for
- Order processing
- Order delivery
- Customer payments
- Inventory acquisitions
- Hiring, managing and compensating employees
- Payments to suppliers

# IPOS cycle

![[IPOS cycle]]

1. Input data, information, knowledge are collected, captured, scanned and snapped from transactions and instructions are defined
2. Processing programs, equipments and storage to change, calculate, manipulate and work with information
3. Output reports, graphics and calculations to show results
	- Feedback communication error report and performance metrics to send results
4. Storage hard drive, server, USB

# Types of information systems

![[Types of information systems]]

## Executive Information Systems

EIS supports executives to identify long-term trends and to design strategic action-plans. It supports unstructured decisions, it's flexible and customizable and it focuses on effectiveness of the organization

EIS uses non-structured data from internal sources (TPS and MIS) and external sources

## Decision Support Systems

DSS is an interactive application that supports the decision-making process by manipulating and building from the information generated by the MIS to create corporate integrated [[knowledge]]

DSS creates main reports ad-hoc reports - Non-planned reports generated in response to a request with the goal of studying a specific situation, problem or opportunity

DSS is oriented to non-structured or semi-structured decision-making, oriented to the future

> [!a] Examples
> Which product to develop
> Which market to enter

Characteristics
- Interactive and easy to use interface
- Use data from multiple sources
- Include models and formulas (statistical, financial, optimization, simulation)
- Decision-makers can manipulate the formulas to make experiments and sensibility analysis
	- What-if analysis: change of prepositions in order to observe the impacts of the changes in the results
	- Goal seeking: calculation of the minimal input necessary to achieve a pre-determined output

> [!a] Example of a DSS
> GPS

## Management Information Systems

MIS is a report system that provides structural [[information]]

It's goal is to provide reports to support management, monitoring and control. It helps managers to assess the company's performance through the comparison with the previous results. It is focused on the past and present. But lacks analytical capabilities

MIS uses TPS data to produce results

Usually, it covers multiple functional areas: human resources, finance, production, logistics, marketing, sales

Main reports
- Programmed/periodic reports
- Reports of exceptions when something that happened was not expected
	- Examples: natural catastrophes, shock in sales

## Transaction Processing Systems

TPS is an efficiency-oriented Information system used to register transactions and update databases, so it can produce information for other ISs

> [!a] Examples
> Sales, payments, orders, wages, stocks

It can be manual by inserting data or automatic through QR codes or bar codes

It is mainly used by frontline, operational workers

Two ways to process transactions
- Real-time processing each transaction is processed individually in real time
- Batch processing multiple transactions are accumulated for a time period and then processed in at a specific time altogether
	- Example: wage process and payment
