Data mining is a technique/tool for the analysis of raw data (either non-structured, semi-structured and/or structured) via specialized software with the aim of classifying data and finding data structures like correlations, anomalies, journeys or significant patterns

> [!a] Main disciplines
> Statistics
> [[Artificial Intelligence]]
> [[Machine Learning]]

# Data mining in organizations

## Benefits

- Anticipate and solve problems
- Plan for the future
- Make informed decisions
- Mitigate risks
- Seize new opportunities to grow

## Process

1. Outlining [[SMART goals]] business goals
2. Understanding the data sources that provide the relevant data needed
3. Preparing the data -> [[ETL process]]
4. Analysing data - identifying patterns and forecasting future trends
5. Reviewing the results
6. Deployment or implementation of the results in order to achieve the business objectives
