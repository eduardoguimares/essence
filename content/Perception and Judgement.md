*Speaking generally a judging observer will tend to seize the conscious character, while a perceptive observer will be influenced more by the unconscious character, since judgment is chiefly interested in the conscious motivation of the psychic process, while perception tends to register the mere happening*[^1]

Judgement - dimension of interpretation
Perception - dimension of space-time

[^1]: Title: *Psychological Types* 
Author: C. G. Jung
Date: 1923
