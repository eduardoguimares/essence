According to the [[SECI knowledge management model]], learning is the conversion of explicit knowledge to tacit knowledge

# Why

- The world is always changing
- *You can’t appreciate what you don’t see*[^3]. Learn about the intricacies of reality and its beauty will be opened to you
- Understand who you are, your nature, values and body, so you can make decisions accordingly to them
- *Over time, the person who approaches life with an openness to being wrong and a willingness to learn outperforms the person who doesn’t*[^4]
# How to improve learning

## Prioritize optimal learning actions

1. Teach (active)
2. Practice (active)
3. Discuss (active)
4. Demonstrate (active)
5. Watch (passive)
6. Read (passive)
7. Listen (passive)

### Teach

By teaching, you are forced to deconstruct your understanding to the most minute detail, so you can construct again, but this time in a simpler way. If the student doesn't understand, you repeat the process. That forces you to understand every single element of a rational construct

## Consume intentionally

Define a goal - what do you want to learn about? - and then search for that information, instead of opening the feed or a book and consuming whatever appeared
