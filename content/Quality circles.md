Regular meetings where a group of workers discuss methods to solve existent problems

It originated in Japan after the World War II

This empowers workers and improves productivity

# The adaptive execution cycle

![[Adaptive execution cycle]]

1. Plan
	- Determine monitoring objectives and information needs
	- Identify key performance indicators
	- Establish a monitoring program
2. Execute
3. Evaluate
	- Data collection and analysis
	- Evaluate information needs and status of performance indicators
	- Report findings of evaluation
	- Periodically review overall monitoring program
4. Adjust execution of work and/or take management actions
