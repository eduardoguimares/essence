Sensemaking is the process in which people give meaning to their experience and perceived reality

# Sensemaking in organizations

- Organization as a psychic prison - it changes individuals' perceptions
- Organization and sensemaking coexist
- Organization emerges from sensemaking
- Organization empowers sensemaking

![[Sensemaking - Karl Weick|700]][^1]

Sensemaking helps people make sense of an event and react to it. People use analogous situations to respond to an event, which reflects in their decisions

## Sensemaking and change in organizations

- Change goes beyond the plan
- Communication in the beginning is essential
- Briefing and debriefing boosts learning
	- Debriefing during the changing process is also beneficial
	- It reflects about what was expected, what happened, what explains the gap, what was unpredictable, errors made
	- Failures need to be recognized in order to be used as a learning opportunity
- What happened before the change also has a big impact

# Sensemaking properties

- **Continuous process** - we are in every moment reading and filtering the external and trying to fit with our own framework of expectations
- **Retrospective** - in the face of a situation, we look back and compare with something similar that already happened
- **Social process** - sensemaking doesn't start and end in the individual because it is strongly influenced by other judgement and perceptions
- **Identity** - sensemaking is based on how we construct an identity
- **Plausibility** - sensemaking tries to seek an explanation that is plausible, instead of seeking the objective truth neutrally
- **Environment enactment** - the environment is a product of sensemaking, therefore our decisions will depend on the idea that we have of the environment
