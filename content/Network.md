## As flow

# Social

People organize themselves in networks where they share similar interests

Networks can be used to achieve goals

Here's a process that can be put in place[^1]
1. Start with the end goal - what do you really want to do
2. Understand the system - what drives behavior
3. Which networks you can use
4. Pick your network tactics
5. Put your plan into action

Networks can be used to spread a message. Even if a message becomes viral (spreads quickly), it's difficult to translate that into game-changing action[^1]
