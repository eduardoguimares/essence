God is the pinnacle of abstraction

%%God is the pinnacle of abstraction

Believing in God is also an instinct. Not an individual instinct but a social instinct. Individual instincts are physical. Social instincts are abstract.

Considering the pyramid of Maslow, at the bottom is the most physical and at the top is the most abstract. With societal priorities, it's the inverse. That means that religion is primarily a form of social connection.

As a society, the tendency is to climb "the pyramid" towards physical priorities. Pyramids, temples, churches, cathedrals, etc.

Subconscious developed - social instincts

Unconscious developed - individual instincts

Somewhere in time, human relationships stop being purely based on utility (individually oriented). People started using stories which led to narratives. And that made humans develop a deeper sense of social connection.

These narratives lead to sensemaking (I prefer perception-construct or a construct of perception)

It's interesting how God can be part of so many thinking processes but still not exist a logical link to the conclusion that God exists

To exist (physical existence or even if God exists metaphysically, having the capacity to influence the physical)

But the idea of God "exists" and that is not irrelevant at all%%
