*A [[data]] silo is a repository of data that's controlled by one department or business unit and isolated from the rest of an organization. (...) Siloed data typically is stored in a standalone system and often is incompatible with other data sets. That makes it hard for users in other parts of the organization to access and use the data*[^1]

It creates the silo effect, when separated departments or teams do not have a system to communicate between themselves. The data gets stuck in departmentalized databases and it affects productivity negatively


[^1]: *What is Data Mining*, Cubeware GmbH (2021)
