*Neurodiversity describes how diverse we are as human beings from a neurological perspective. It suggests that the many variations of human brain, like autistic ones, should be accepted as a natural and valuable part of humanity's and genetic legacy*[^1]

# Types

- Dyslexia - affects the ability to read and write accurately
- Dyspraxia - affects how the mind processes actions, usually affecting coordination and movement
- ADD/ADHD (Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder) - is characterized by inattentiveness, impulsivity and hyperactivity
- Autism spectrum - condition which affects how people interact and communicate with the world
- Tourette syndrome

[^1]: *Neurodiversity – the key that unlocked my world*, Elisabeth Wiklander (2017)
