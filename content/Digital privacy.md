# Why would anyone care?

***But I'm not doing anything wrong, therefore I have nothing to hide***

The problem is not you doing something wrong. The problem is a third party doing something wrong with what they know about you

## Just like any other type of privacy

For the same reason anyone would want to keep some intimate information from any other people

## Security

Passwords... this one is obvious

The less personal information is on the [[Internet]], the more secure one's accounts and life is going to be. Data breaches happen every time. Hackers can use that information (even regular info like names, services that you use, personal interests, or worst, credentials) to scam, steal identities, social engineer. etc.
