Design thinking is a user-centric, solutions-based approach to problem-solving that emphasizes understanding the user’s needs and developing [[Innovation|Innovative]] solutions

The common two forces in every design thinking model -> [[Convergence-Divergence]]

# Stages of design thinking

1. Clarify understanding the problem in question
2. Brainstorming of potential solutions - [[Intermingleness]]
3. Develop testing and iterating the generated solutions to determine which are successful
4. Implement
