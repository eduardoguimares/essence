# What is strategy

- A pattern in a set of decisions[^3]
- Determination of the long-term goals of an organization and the adoption of the actions and allocation of the necessary resources to achieve those goals[^1]
- Strategy is being different. It means choose deliberately a set of activities to obtain an unique mix of value[^2]

Additionally, strategy is the long-term approach of an organization and not the long-term achievements. It is what allows an organization to sustain and grow[^4]

# Strategy along the years

- 1st focus - defend the core business and expand
- 2nd focus - create emergent business
- 3rd focus - create viable options

# Strategy levels

Strategy has a more micro and macro component even thought strategy is usually more associated with corporate strategy
- Corporate strategy - how to add value to the business units and organization
	- Top management
- Business (or competitive) strategy - less focused on the direction of the organization and more focused on how a business unit can be more successful in its market
	- Middle management
- Operational strategy - how people, resources and processes are being managed in order to support the business and corporate strategy

## Which can be applied to our personal life

- Strategy at the corporate level: what value do we want to add to the world?
- Strategy at the business level: how we can improve an area of our lives
- Strategy at the operational level: how to have efficient habits

# [[Strategy declaration]]

- Purpose, vision, mission, goals and values
- Context of the activities
- Unique position (advantages and skills available) that one has to achieve its goals and objectives

# Exploring strategy model

3 main elements

## Strategic position

- What unique competences the organization has?
- What's its purpose?
- What is and how is the [[Organizational environment]]?
- What is the culture of the organization?

## Strategic choices

- How should the business units compete?
- Which businesses to include?
- Which markets to compete?
- How to continue to innovate?
- Should we acquire another company or form an alliance?

## Strategy in action

- How is the strategy being implemented
- Management questions:
	- Processes
	- Evaluation
	- Organization
	- Practice
	- Change

# Strategy management

- Communication - Strategy should be very well communicated to everyone
- Contribution - Everyone should be able to contribute to the strategy, by presenting ideas
- Internal unit - in big organizations there are internal job functions dedicated to strategic planning and analysis
- External help - strategic consultants could be contracted
	- There has been a growing number of consulting firms specialized in strategy

# [[Strategic competences]]

Strategic competences are competences that position the organization in an unique position and allow the organization to get a competitive advantage
