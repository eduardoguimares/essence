How people make choices in the face of scarcity. There are infinite wants but finite resources. Economics seeks to explain how people, organizations and societies make trade-offs

# How individuals make choices

## Common exercise when learning economic theory

Let's say you have a 18€ budget. You can choose between two products: a burger and a beverage. A burger costs 6€ and a beverage costs 3€

In this example, we presuppose that you will spend all your budget in the current moment since economics takes for granted that you want to maximize your material well-being and adding the option to save money would complexify the example to much for now

This is a representation of the budget constraint. You can either choose to buy
- 3 burgers + 0 beverages
- 2 burgers + 2 beverages
- 1 burger + 4 beverages
- 0 burgers + 6 beverages
