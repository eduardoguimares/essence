OLAP *is software technology you can use to analyze business data from different points of view. Organizations collect and store data from multiple data sources, such as websites, applications, smart meters, and internal systems. OLAP combines and groups this data into categories to provide actionable insights for strategic planning*[^1]

> [!a] Example
Retailer store collecting data about the product they sell (color, size, cost, location) and data about purchases (sales value, name of the items) and OLAP combining that data to help answer important questions like 'Which color products are more popular' or 'How product placement impact sale'[^1]
