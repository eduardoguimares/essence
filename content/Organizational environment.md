# Scenario planning

It's important to be always aware of the possible business landscapes and how they might affect the organization. These scenarios guide organizations in the decision making process, help create the SWOT analysis and allows the coexistence with uncertainties.
Scenario planning process:
1. Identify driving forces
2. Identify critical uncertainties
3. Develop plausible scenarios
4. Discuss implications and paths and one's probabilities
# Industry and sector

## Porter's 5 forces model

The [[Porter's 5 forces model]] identifies and analyzes five competitive forces that shape every industry and helps determine an industry's weaknesses and strengths

- Competition
- Threat of new entrants to the industry
- Supplier bargaining power
- Customer bargaining power
	- Particularly the [[Strategic clients]]
- The ability of customers to find substitutes

## Industry life cycle

| Market size           | Emergence                                       | Growth                                       | Maturity                                          | Decline                                 |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Indicators            | High growth potential, limited or no regulation | Fragmentation, shapeable regulation          | Low growth, high concentration, stable regulation | No growth. restricted financing losses  |
| Rivalry among players | Low (high product differentiation)              | Increasing (weak buyers, low entry barriers) | Strong (stronger buyers, higher entry barriers)   | Extreme (many exits, price competition) |
| Strategy focus        | Innovation                                      | Ability to grow                              | Market share, lower costs                         | Ramp down disengage                     |

# Market competitors

## Strategic groups

Organizations that present similar strategies

Characteristics that can be similar:
- Diversity of the products
- Geographic coverage
- Market segments
- Distribution channels
- Number of brands
- Marketing effort
- Vertical integration how extensive
- Product quality
- Technological position
- Dimension of the organization

## Market segments

A [[Market segments|market segment]] set of buyers with similar necessities. They tend to have a set of characteristics that differentiate between buyers from other segments

## Blue oceans

New market spaces where the competition is minimized and there's a lot of growth potential

The Blue Ocean strategy requires the addition of the [[Time context]] perspective to the market analysis
