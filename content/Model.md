There are no accurate models, but some are useful[^1]

The purpose of models is not to explain life but to provide a timely insight to the [[decision making]] process

Just like an economic model that allows the central bank to decide when and by how much to change the interest rate. Or just like a company that has an optimization model that gives an insight to which price the company should attach to a product. Or just like a bank that employs a predictive model that helps them decide if they should concede credit to an individual
