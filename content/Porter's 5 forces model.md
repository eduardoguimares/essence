A [[model]] that identifies and analyzes five competitive forces that shape every industry and helps determine an industry's weaknesses and strengths

![[Illustration of Porter's 5 forces.svg|700]]

# Threat of new entrants

The competitiveness of the industry increases with the threat of new entrants

The threat of new entrants increases when
- Economy of scale is not very much present
- It is not necessary a lot of experience, highly complex competences or big investments to enter in the industry
- The companies already at the industry do not retaliate (through price reduction of publicity)
- The government doesn't protect the industry with legislation (licensing, taxes...)
- Nothing in particular differentiates the companies (geographic location, patents...)

# Threat of substitute products

The competitiveness of the industry increases with the threat of substitute products (offered by same industry or not)

The threat of substitute products increases when:
- The substitute products offer better performance (even if more expensive)
- The cost of changing products is low

# Bargaining power of buyers

It increases with
- Less buyers (more concentration)
- Low cost of changing products
- Similar products (buyers can easily change products)
- Vertical integration - when the buyer has the capacity of producing the product

# Bargaining power of suppliers

It increases with:
- Less suppliers (more concentration)
- High cost of changing suppliers
- The necessity of an unique/specialized product
- Vertical integration - when the supplier has the capacity of integrating the product that he produces into his supply chain or sell to the final costumer

# Industry competitiveness

It increases with:
- More competitors
- More balanced competitors (similar size and power)
- Mature and declining industries
- High fixed costs
- High exit costs
- Low product differentiation (clients easily change products)

# Other questions
The model should be applied to the correct level of industry not necessarily to the whole industry

It is necessary to understand if there are any convergent industries or complementary organizations (which increase the attractiveness of a business)

# Criticisms of the model

It can be too broad and general to some industries
