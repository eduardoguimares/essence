> [!a]- Note
> This theoretical framework is mostly based on Carl Jung and much less on Freud because *for a theory whose validity is incontestable for the psyche from which it originated proves itself worthless and even misleading for an individual of another type*[^6]

> [!b] A personality type is the set of cognitive tendencies of the human brain

A personality type is the combination of [[Cognitive function]]s and [[Archetype]]s that operate within the sides of the mind ([[Ego]], [[Subconscious]], [[Unconscious]] and [[Super-ego]])
